# Template art direction, October 12

Reading this as seven business websites for prospective customers, with photograph-led compositions and useful enquiry tools. Energy 3 / rhythm 3 / motion 3 at the opening; reading and forms settle to motion 1.

The user's newest references point to three qualities: an immersive focal image, layered compositions that introduce the actual offering, and confidently contrasting chapters. Copying the blue orbit of dashboard cards into every sector would miss the brief. The subject and the movement must belong to each business.

| Family | Signature and purpose | Motion | Practical journey |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Architecture | A panoramic living-space photograph, monumental type, a measured vertical frame and an anchored discipline rail. Light and spatial proportion are the subject. | Slow camera drift and measured line reveal. | Selected spaces, project story, process, renovation or commercial brief. |
| Padel | Wide condensed court typography above a photographic court window; a contrasting session rail beside the image. It reads as a sports club rather than software. | A ball crosses the court diagram; image has a restrained lateral pan. | Find a playing format, coaching or membership, prepare a first visit. |
| Creative | Dark studio stage, oversized restrained headline and a fan of cropped process images. Each panel names the actual discipline and links to its work. | A staggered fan settles into place, then a small opposing sway. | Work, identity/digital case stories, packaging and a project brief. |
| Consulting | Blue photographic atmosphere with centered copy and a horizontal decision dock. The dock links to business strategy, operations and workshops. | A decision line progresses beneath stable copy; photograph moves very slowly. | Understand expertise, examine approach, prepare evidence, request discussion. |
| Hospitality | A place-led photographic opening with an inset detail, followed by room comparison and practical stay information. | Quiet camera motion. | Compare rooms, view gallery, dates/guest enquiry, arrival and dining information. |
| Catering | A food composition on aubergine with staggered crops and menu-like rows, avoiding hotel or corporate layouts. | Food photographs settle at different angles. | Filter menus, compare event services, send guest count/date/dietary needs. |
| Logistics | A port panorama with a legible operational dispatch panel. Navy and ice surfaces organize cargo information. | A route travels between handover points. | Choose freight/storage/distribution, calculate volume, prepare quote requirements. |

Typography stays sector-specific. Large type is concentrated in the opening; detail pages use readable headings and a quieter rhythm. Photographic gradients protect text, not decorate every surface. Depth separates work previews from the background; form fields and reading sections remain flat. Each core action uses the family's one accent, with explicit visible focus.

The fan and layered compositions use real existing photographs and CMS fields. They introduce actual disciplines, not invented customer metrics or live dashboards. All static-page enquiry forms review a message before the visitor chooses WhatsApp or email; they do not pretend to confirm bookings or quotes. The page-by-page use-case audit lives alongside this direction.

Self-critique before implementation: seven split heroes would be monotonous; seven animated card fans would copy the reference instead of interpreting it. Only Creative uses a fan, Architecture uses spatial framing, Padel uses a court window, and Consulting uses a decision dock. Content pages inherit the typography and section rhythm, while task controls remain conventional and predictable.
