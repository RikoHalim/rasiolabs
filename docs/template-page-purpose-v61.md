# Purpose of all 70 template pages

Photo galleries, filters, topic tabs and forms share tested behavior rather than duplicating unconnected controls. All routes are reachable from their family home, approved in the CMS navigation model and rewritten to the client's own base by the Worker renderer. Public preview statistics are owner-requested examples; new client statistics remain disabled until supplied.
