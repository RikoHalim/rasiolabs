# Hospitality and Catering: visual and purpose revision

## Design read

Anti Slop is applied during design, following the user's explicit session choice. The latest references call for a memorable photographic hero, layered depth, restrained typography, and purposeful movement. These are company-profile templates with enquiry tools, not booking engines.

Hospitality uses a place-led pool panorama, a framed bathroom detail, oversized white serif type, and forest / pale mineral chapters. The hero introduces the destination and leads to room selection. The image inset adds evidence of the interior finish, not another marketing claim. Energy 3, rhythm 3, motion 2.

Catering uses an aubergine stage, generous food photography, a staggered plated-food inset and pale citrus actions. Menu rows read like a menu, while occasion pages lead to a preselected enquiry. Its controlled image drift suggests a table coming together, distinct from the hotel's slow camera movement. Energy 3, rhythm 3, motion 2.

Text and buttons remain stationary. Hero content is visible before JavaScript. Responsive layouts remove overlap before reducing the reading size. Reduced motion removes decorative animation. Existing CMS fields, filters, gallery controls, topic handoffs and form validation are preserved.
