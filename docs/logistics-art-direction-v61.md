# Logistics: movement, made clear

Subject: a freight and storage company serving operations teams. The homepage helps a visitor choose freight, storage or distribution, then prepare an actionable enquiry.

## Visual plan

- Port navy `#0d2637`, reading ink `#142f40`, ice `#eef4f7`, white `#ffffff`, cargo amber `#ffc56d`, muted steel `#526a78`.
- Archivo Narrow carries large transport-signage headings; Archivo carries requirements, labels and forms. No fabricated metrics or tracking screens.
- Signature: a working port panorama behind a stable headline, with a raised handover panel. Its moving line represents collection-to-delivery coordination, and its links open real service pages.
- Homepage rhythm: immersive panorama; compact service navigation; short company introduction; offset service rows; warehouse photograph and storage brief; enquiry.
- Subpages use quieter task-specific compositions: photo service catalogue, freight timeline plus calculator, warehouse receiving-to-dispatch flow, distribution tabs, annotated cargo guidance, checklist, questions, company principles and enquiry.
- ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2. Motion stays on the photograph and handover line, with restrained below-fold image entries. Text and inputs remain still. Reduced-motion disables decorative motion.

Plan critique: a generic three-card logistics dashboard would imply live software that the template does not provide. The revised concept uses the actual port and warehouse as its subject, and service links plus enquiry tools as its interaction. The navy surface isolates photographic atmosphere; white/ice surfaces preserve practical reading contrast.

## Page and section use cases

| Page | Visitor task | Useful sections and next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Identify available logistics support | Service links, handover explanation, storage overview, shipment enquiry |
| Freight & storage | Compare service categories | Filterable freight, storage and distribution offerings; cargo preparation resources |
| Freight planning | Prepare a transport request | Shipment/movement/handover sequence; package-volume calculator; enquiry |
| Warehousing | Describe receiving, stock and dispatch needs | Receiving-to-dispatch operations flow; storage brief; storage enquiry |
| Distribution | Choose one delivery or a recurring route | Keyboard-accessible delivery-pattern tabs; handover requirements; preselected enquiry |
| Cargo guide | Measure, protect and identify packages | Four practical topics; links to calculator and checklist |
| Shipment preparation | Gather the information a provider needs | Working checklist with progress; enquiry and cargo guide |
| Shipment questions | Resolve common pre-enquiry questions | Expandable answers; shipment enquiry |
| The company | Understand how responsibilities are agreed | Approach, responsibilities, information and planning; enquiry |
| Shipment enquiry | Prepare a reviewable shipment request | Contact, route, date, goods, package count, weight, dimensions and instructions; reviewed contact handoff |

Client content remains CMS-editable. No availability, confirmed capacity, transport price, delivery guarantee or live shipment status is asserted. The reviewed enquiry is a contact handoff, not a confirmed transport booking.
