## Design purpose

Olive and warm cream reflect a table-and-kitchen editorial character. Newsreader supplies expressive food-magazine headings, while Hanken Grotesk keeps menu notes and the event form legible. The layout uses menu rows, offset photographs, and a flat navigation bar rather than repeating the other templates' capsule header and card arrangement.

ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2. A finite image-aperture reveal introduces food and kitchen photography; menu filtering makes the collection useful rather than a static gallery. Reduced motion keeps every image and piece of copy visible. No invented testimonial, business performance statistic, team identity, booking confirmation, or payment flow appears in this template.

Menus are editable template content, not a claim about the dishes offered by an existing catering business. Prospective clients can replace all headings, dishes, service descriptions, and images through the CMS fields. Dietary copy asks visitors to discuss allergies and preparation; it makes no allergen-free guarantee.
