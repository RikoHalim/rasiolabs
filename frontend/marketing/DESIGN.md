## Intentional visual choices

- DM Sans is reused and self-hosted. Its open geometric forms and moderate weight support the owner's Hostinger-inspired clarity without adopting that brand's type or purple palette.
- Main ink #142640, action blue #1454ed, supporting ice #edf3ff and navy #101f3c. White and neutral border shades support these colors. Green identifies included-benefit checks and the Custom WhatsApp consultation action. The original logo artwork stays unchanged.
- A restrained blue wash behind the hero connects the actual template previews to the brand. A much quieter tint groups the price amount. Other sections use solid surfaces: white, ice, light blue and navy. No decorative spheres, glow system or background grid.
- Section backgrounds separate tasks: ice for managed-service explanation, cool off-white for service previews, warm off-white for browsing templates, soft blue for the ordering steps, navy for AI, and cool off-white for pricing. White template cards with a continuous border distinguish each choice from the catalogue background without heavy shadows.
- The identity motif is the website frame: actual existing template screenshots recur in the hero, service composition and catalogue. The decorative hero is not clickable. There are no generic people photos in the services.
- Hero is asymmetric with Padel at the front of the existing three-template stack. Essentials form a compact strip; responsibility is a split panel; product tabs contain template frames, a storefront and a connected workflow. The existing catalogue has visible preview buttons on every card. Process is a five-stage rail; AI has three equal-priority illustrated cards and one chatbot strip. Pricing uses three cards: light blue Profile, navy Store, neutral Custom. Per the owner's 21 October correction, desktop pricing cards have equal outer heights and share badge, header, price, CTA, and benefit rows through CSS subgrid. A grouped benefits wrapper preserves the existing footer notes and Custom proposal steps. Below 961px the cards stack and return to natural content height. Unsupported browsers retain the flex fallback. Trust uses open columns; FAQs use native disclosure. Dashboard showcase is removed.
- 1184px content width, 56–88px section spacing, 48px button height, 10px button radius, 20px cards and 28px large panels. Rounded floating navigation is retained per the owner. The hero gets viewport-aware space without artificial whitespace on phones.
- No fake customer logos, endorsements, ratings, conversion lifts or business performance claims. The small analytics number is explicitly an illustrative interface example. All prices come from the approved policy module. AI and Store retain honest launch availability.
- Gradients only distinguish the hero, layered Profile previews, and price header. The navbar is the only translucent surface. Product-frame shadows establish layer order; other panels use borders. Icons describe hosting, mail, domain, editing, search and chat. Arrows identify preview/consultation destinations; they are not added to every primary CTA. Equal AI card sizes reflect three peer features, while their illustrations remain distinct.

## Behavior contracts

- No new picker, template detail page or checkout is implemented. “Pilih Template” and “Lihat Template” retain service, stable template ID and selected duration through the existing v4 summary and live preview. Both remain visible on mobile; thumbnails also carry the selection. URL state survives refresh, history, and native new-tab navigation without shared storage. Search/filter is retained for future catalogue growth but hidden with the single active Padel template; it has clear/reset, empty state and URL query state. With a larger catalogue, six results appear initially; “Muat lebih banyak” reveals the next six matches and disappears when all matches are visible. Filtering resets the visible limit. The newly revealed choice receives keyboard focus without a forced scroll. The future scalable visual picker remains deferred.
- Product tabs support arrow keys, Home/End and focus, and restore the selected service from the URL on history/refresh. A deliberate service change retains duration and clears a template only when it no longer belongs to that service. They do not auto-rotate; their height is reserved per viewport to avoid document jumps. AI has no tabs, demo buttons or interactive states. No screen-wide transition or refresh fade.
- `service-links.js` carries the existing URL selection into and out of the detail routes. Explicit package links select their stated duration; navigation retains the relevant service, template, and duration without replacing WEB-01 validation or its existing summary. Profile catalogue/preview actions continue using Padel. Store has no selectable template; its disabled catalogue control does not activate a new order path or catalogue entry. `service-page.js` reuses navigation and motion initialization. Policy links remain in the footer.
- Pricing uses native radio controls, shows equivalent monthly price plus paid-up-front total, removes the crossed-out price for one month, and switches domain eligibility, savings and badges. The link preserves selected duration and opens the existing v4 package/checkout preview. The requested ordering-status labels are removed from marketing. Actual production payment remains disabled in the existing checkout.
- Floating hero: 8px slow vertical travel, max 2.5° pointer tilt on fine-pointer devices only. Animation pauses offscreen/inactive. Scroll reveal is one-time and progressive. Process line reveals once. Per the owner's 18 October request, AI chart, SEO scan and revised-text illustrations loop on 8–9.6-second cycles, with a readable hold and a quiet reset. Each pauses when its card is offscreen or the tab is inactive; reduced motion shows the complete static illustration. The loops explain the three features without calling an AI service. Price cards do not move on hover.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables animations and smooth scroll; pointer tilt is reset when the preference changes. Buttons have hover, focus-visible and active states. Menus close on navigation, Escape and outside click.
- No JavaScript: main copy, Padel template buttons, initial price, links and FAQ remain readable; search is hidden until initialized. Changing service tabs and duration requires JavaScript. The initial price is explicitly the 48-month plan.
